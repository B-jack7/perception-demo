# Data provenance

`calvin_unstack_before.jpg` and `calvin_unstack_after.jpg` are the stacked and unstacked views used by the demo. They were downloaded from row 62 of the public `ShuaKang/calvin_abc_d_val` Hugging Face dataset mirror, whose text instruction is **“unstack the blocks”**.

- Mirror: <https://huggingface.co/datasets/ShuaKang/calvin_abc_d_val>
- Original CALVIN benchmark and license information: <https://github.com/mees/calvin>
- Retrieval date: 2026-11-04

The files in `candidates/` are neighboring task examples retained only for optional experiments. The main demo does not train on or manually annotate these images.
